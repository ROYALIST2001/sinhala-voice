Step 02 · Get comfortable

A few things before we start.

No perfect pronunciation needed. Just be yourself.

  • Find a quiet spot

    Keep the TV, music and other conversations low.

  • Hold your phone naturally

    Keep it near your mouth, as you would on a call.

  • Tap. Wait for the beep. Speak.

    Say the displayed word once. Recording stops automatically after 2 seconds.

  • One word, ten separate takes

    Then 5 silent clips and 5 random Sinhala sentences. These last up to 3 seconds each.
